Talk to a care advisor

  • Health checks and screenings

    Regular assessments that catch problems early: blood work, heart, bones, eyesight and more, arranged at a place and time that suits your parents.

  • Doctors and specialists

    Consultations with general physicians and specialists, online or in person, with notes shared with the family after every visit.

  • Chronic condition care

    Steady support for diabetes, blood pressure, arthritis and other long-term conditions, with follow-ups so nothing slips.

  • Medicine reminders and refills

    A simple routine for the right dose at the right time, and refills ordered before the strip runs out.

  • Emergency coordination

    When something goes wrong, we help arrange the nearest hospital, ambulance and paperwork while you are on your way.

  • Physiotherapy and wellness at home

    Gentle exercise plans, physiotherapy sessions, companionship visits and lifestyle guidance that keep seniors active and independent.

Questions families ask us

Cannot find your answer? Call or message us and a care advisor will help.

Who is Nityaseva for?

Adults aged 45 and above, and especially parents and grandparents whose families want reliable support, whether they live nearby or in another city or country.

Do you replace my parents' own doctor?

No. We work alongside their doctors, help with appointments and reports, and add specialists when needed.

Can I manage everything from another city?

Yes. You get updates after every check-up or visit, and one advisor you can call for anything.

What happens in an emergency?

Call India's emergency number 112 first. Then call us, and we will help with hospital coordination, family updates and paperwork.
