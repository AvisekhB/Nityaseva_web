How Nityaseva works

Four steps. Choose one to see what happens.

Tell us about your parent

A short, friendly conversation to understand their health, daily routine and what worries you most. No sales pressure.

  • Their health history and current medicines
  • Whether they live alone, with family or with an attendant
  • The one thing you would most like taken off your mind

Meet your care executive

A trained care executive is introduced to you and to your parent, so the first visit feels familiar rather than formal.

  • One consistent person, not a different face every time
  • A visit schedule that suits your parent's day
  • A direct way for you to reach them

Regular visits, real reports

At each visit the care executive records how your parent is doing and shares it with you the same day.

  • Wellbeing, meals, medication and safety logged
  • Blood pressure, pulse and sugar recorded every time
  • Changes noticed early, before they become a crisis

Help when it matters

If something goes wrong, you have one number to call. Someone who already knows your parent gets things moving.

  • Ambulance, hospital and doctor coordinated for you
  • Family kept informed at every step
  • For a life-threatening emergency, always call 112 first

Questions families ask us

Cannot find your answer? Message us and we will reply personally.

Where do you work?

We are starting in Asansol, West Bengal. If you live elsewhere, leave your number and we will call you when we launch in your area.

Who is Nityaseva for?

Families whose parents or grandparents live at home and need someone reliable to check in, whether the family lives nearby or in another city.

What does a care executive do?

They visit on a schedule, record blood pressure, pulse and sugar, check that medicines are taken, note how your parent is feeling and eating, and report back to you the same day.

Do you replace my parent's doctor?

No. We work alongside their doctors and help with appointments, records and follow-ups.

What happens in an emergency?

Call India's emergency number 112 first. Then call us, and we will help coordinate the ambulance, hospital and family.
